The land

Sea, river, waterfall, forest.
All of it, inside one boundary.

01

A private beach on the open Atlantic

Golden sand where the river meets the sea, granite boulders, clear water. Facing east toward the open ocean, with no neighbours on the horizon.

02

A river, a waterfall, and water you can drink

The property holds its own spring — 100% protected, rising inside forest that will never be cleared. Fresh water runs down through pools and cascades to the beach.

03

Mata Atlântica, on every side

One of the most biodiverse forests on Earth — and one of the most threatened. Here it runs unbroken from the ridgeline to the shore. About 2 km of trails already wind through it, with room for many more.

04

Coffee and cacao, grown in the shade

Plantations of coffee and cacao already grow under the forest canopy — the beginning of a working, regenerative estate with its own story to tell, from bean to cup.
